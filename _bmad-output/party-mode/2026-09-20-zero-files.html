Step three · ruled as of five verbatims, understating on purpose

Q3 — does the field challenge the vision?
No. It is unstated,
unenforced, and unbuilt.
  • Unstated — the doctrine appears in zero files. A strategy nobody can read cannot be followed.
  • Unenforced — every speaker pulled toward specificity. Nothing makes generic the easy path. There is only advice, delivered in person, in a workshop.
  • Unbuilt — RAG, retrieval, knowledge engineering: zero rows. The load path of the whole strategy has one March intake that says “potential”.
  • And one gap — self-contained versus connected was never decided. Inherited defaults are the most dangerous kind: you defend them as if you'd chosen them.
Q4 — is BMAD-on-top still relevant?
Two of three.
More relevant, not less.
  • Not cleared — install topology is the clean candidate at 2 of 3. Version coupling at 1. Supply chain split: the rating inherited, the distribution ours.
  • The inversion — “keep it generic so open-source contributors maintain it” is an argument for living in somebody else's commons. The strategy requires a BMAD.
  • What's failing — not the relationship. Being silent inside it: invisible in the host's own help, not following it above the repo layer, holding 6.12 with no policy.
  • Victor, dissenting, recorded intact — relevance is not settled by a threshold. You can be architecturally correct and structurally unadoptable.
