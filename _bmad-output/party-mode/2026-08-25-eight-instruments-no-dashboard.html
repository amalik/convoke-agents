What was actually learned

Store only what the author alone knows. Derive or reference everything else.
Three status axes are real. Only one is a property of the document.
Four documents independently invented a status field, because none of them had one.
In _bmad-output/: a thousand stale files and nothing that can archive them.
In docs/: the exact inverse — the governance is attached to the wrong half.
📐AtlasModel Curator
